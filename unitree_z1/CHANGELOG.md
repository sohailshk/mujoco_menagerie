# Changelog – Unitree Z1 Description

All notable changes to this model will be documented in this file.

## [2026-11-10]
- Added `armature="0.005"` to `joint5` and `joint6` (`z1.xml`, `z1_gripper.xml`). With the 1000/100 position gains and no armature, the wrist actuators chattered between the +/-30 force limits and could not reach their targets.
